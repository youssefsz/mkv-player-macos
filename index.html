MKV playback,
made for Mac.

QuickTime Player doesn’t open MKV files. MKV Player does—with native macOS controls and hardware-accelerated playback. MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI, TS, and M2TS work too.

Download MKV Player v0.1.1 · macOS 14+

MKV first.
Not MKV only.

Built for the format gap on macOS. Open an MKV with its video, audio tracks, subtitles, and chapters intact—no conversion or codec packs. The same focused player handles other common formats too.

MKV
Matroska video, audio tracks, subtitles, and chapters
Also plays
MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI, TS, and M2TS
Playback
libmpv with VideoToolbox hardware decoding
Mac
Native AppKit interface for Apple Silicon and Intel

Install

Open the disk image, drag MKV Player into Applications, then open an MKV file or press Command-O.
